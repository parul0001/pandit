What we perform

Services & Sanskars

Every ceremony includes samagri guidance, muhurat selection and a clear explanation of each step — so the family understands what is being offered and why.

01
गृह प्रवेश
Griha Pravesh
◷ 3–4 hours

Housewarming ceremony to purify a new home, invoke Vastu Purush and settle auspicious energies before the family moves in.

  • Vastu shanti & navagraha puja
  • Kalash sthapana
  • Havan and purnahuti
  • Muhurat selection
02
सत्यनारायण कथा
Satyanarayan Katha
◷ 2–3 hours

The complete five-chapter katha with sankalp, puja and prasad distribution — performed for gratitude, vows fulfilled and family wellbeing.

  • Sankalp & ganesh pujan
  • Five adhyaya recitation
  • Aarti & prasad vidhi
  • Panchamrit preparation
03
विवाह संस्कार
Vivah Sanskar
◷ Full day

Traditional Hindu wedding conducted with every step explained to the couple and families, from mandap puja to the saptapadi.

  • Ganesh & matri pujan
  • Kanyadaan and panigrahan
  • Saptapadi with meanings
  • Kundli milan on request
04
नामकरण संस्कार
Namkaran Sanskar
◷ 1–2 hours

Naming ceremony for the newborn, with nakshatra-based syllable guidance and blessings for a long, healthy life.

  • Nakshatra & rashi calculation
  • Suggested name syllables
  • Surya darshan vidhi
  • Blessing havan
05
हवन एवं यज्ञ
Havan & Yagna
◷ 2–5 hours

Fire rituals for peace, prosperity and removal of doshas — from a simple ghar havan to Rudrabhishek and Navagraha shanti.

  • Rudrabhishek
  • Navagraha shanti
  • Mahamrityunjaya jaap
  • Sundarkand path
06
कुंडली एवं ज्योतिष
Kundli & Jyotish
◷ 45–60 min

Birth-chart preparation and reading, muhurat selection for important events, and practical remedies rooted in the shastras.

  • Janam kundli making
  • Kundli milan
  • Muhurat for events
  • Dosha upay & remedies
07
श्राद्ध एवं पितृ कार्य
Shraddh & Pitra Karya
◷ 2–4 hours

Ancestral rites performed with dignity — shraddh, tarpan and pind daan observed according to family tradition.

  • Tarpan & pind daan
  • Shraddh on tithi
  • Pitra dosha nivaran
  • Antim sanskar guidance
08
मुंडन संस्कार
Mundan Sanskar
◷ 1–2 hours

The first tonsure ceremony, performed on an auspicious day with the accompanying puja and blessings for the child.

  • Muhurat selection
  • Ganesh & kul devta pujan
  • Chudakarma vidhi
  • Prasad & aarti

About the pandit

Pandit Parul

Trained in the guru-shishya tradition and rooted in the Karmakand school, Pandit Parul has spent more than eighteen years performing Vedic ceremonies for families in Ghumarwin and the wider Bilaspur region.

The approach is simple: the ritual is done correctly, and the family understands it. Sankalp, mantra and offering are explained in Hindi and Pahari, so a ceremony becomes something a household participates in rather than watches.

From a newborn's namkaran to a home's griha pravesh, from weddings to ancestral rites, each occasion is prepared for individually — the panchang consulted, the muhurat fixed, the samagri listed well before the day.

Four commitments
